# Final Risk Closure Plan

Date: 2026-06-04

This table separates risks that are already controlled by engineering from risks that require ground truth or manual review. Current evidence is based on the final regression outputs in `output/verification_FORM1_FINAL/`, `output/verification_FORM2_FINAL/`, and `output/verification_FORM3_FINAL/`.

| Risk ID | Risk description | Severity | Impact on grade | Current evidence | Can be solved automatically? | Required input from user/team | Exact file/path needed | Exact format needed | Minimum sample size needed | Proposed fix | Command to run | Expected output | How to verify it is solved | Remaining limitation after fix |

| R1_PRINTED_OCR_ALIGNMENT | PAGE-01 printed OCR crops are often empty, border-contaminated, or low confidence. | high | Printed fields in `PAGE-01` may be wrong even though workbooks are generated. | `printed_ocr_debug.csv`: FORM1 `ok=19/258`, FORM2 `ok=18/312`, FORM3 `ok=25/276`; many `invalid_crop`, `failed`, `low_confidence`. | partial | Ground-truth text for printed PAGE-01 fields. | `annotations/printed_ocr_ground_truth.csv` | CSV columns: `form,pdf_file,page,field_name,ground_truth_text,comment` | At least 10 PDFs per FORM; best is all PAGE-01 PDFs. | Use selected low-confidence/invalid samples to tune crop padding or report OCR limitation with true accuracy. | `python3 scripts/select_review_samples.py`; then `python3 scripts/evaluate_with_ground_truth.py --task printed_ocr --annotations annotations/printed_ocr_ground_truth.csv` | Review sample CSV, printed OCR accuracy, field-level accuracy, error cases. | Exact/normalized accuracy improves or limitations are quantified with real labels. | Without labels, only crop quality and OCR status can be audited, not true OCR accuracy. |
| R2_HANDWRITTEN_NO_GT | Handwritten recognizer has no real exam ground truth. | high | Cannot claim real handwritten accuracy in report. | Real crop audit exists: FORM1 `261`, FORM2 `462`, FORM3 `561` handwritten records; statuses are audit-only. | no | Labels for real handwritten mantisse/exposant/unit crops. | `annotations/handwritten_ground_truth.csv` | CSV columns: `form,pdf_file,page,field_name,crop_id,ground_truth_value,comment` | Start with 100 failed/low-confidence crops; best is all selected review samples. | Evaluate real accuracy and identify whether failures are crop, preprocessing, or model limits. | `python3 scripts/select_review_samples.py`; then `python3 scripts/evaluate_with_ground_truth.py --task handwritten --annotations annotations/handwritten_ground_truth.csv` | Accuracy, per-field accuracy, failed crop rate, confusion matrix, error cases. | Accuracy is computed on labeled real crops and failure modes are linked to debug images. | Even labeled samples may not cover every writer/style unless sample size is broad. |
| R3_FORM3_HANDWRITTEN_FAILURES | FORM3 has many handwritten failed cases. | high | Numeric-answer reliability is weakest on FORM3. | FORM3 handwritten: `561` rows, `failed=206`, `low_confidence=43`, crop quality mostly `border_contamination=401`. | partial | Ground truth for selected FORM3 failed/low-confidence crops; optional manual crop-quality comments. | `annotations/handwritten_ground_truth.csv` plus `annotations/review_samples/handwritten_review_samples.csv` | Same handwritten CSV; focus on rows where `form=FORM3`. | Minimum 50 FORM3 failed cases; recommended 120 selected samples. | Prioritize FORM3 failure review, then tune border removal only if labels show recoverable digits. | `python3 scripts/select_review_samples.py --forms FORM3 --max-per-task 120` and evaluate handwritten. | FORM3-focused review queue and measured error categories. | Solved if failed/low-confidence cases either improve after a tested crop fix or are documented as missing/ambiguous content. | Without labels, automatic code changes risk overfitting and cannot prove improvement. |

| R5_QCM_CANDIDATE_REVIEW | QCM accepted/rejected candidates still need manual review for false positives/false negatives. | medium | Wrong QCM boxes can affect `EXAM` choices. | Candidate logs exist: FORM1 `14205`, FORM2 `21900`, FORM3 `17557`; accepted boxes and rejection reasons are stored with debug images. | partial | Review labels for selected candidates/questions. | `annotations/qcm_review.csv` | CSV columns: `form,pdf_file,page,question_id,expected_choice,comment`; for candidate-level review, use `expected_choice=accepted` or `rejected`. | At least 50 accepted candidates and 50 suspicious rejected candidates per FORM. | Review abnormal accepted boxes, numeric-box rejections, and close-threshold cases; adjust low-level rules only after measured errors. | `python3 scripts/select_review_samples.py`; then `python3 scripts/evaluate_with_ground_truth.py --task qcm --annotations annotations/qcm_review.csv` | False positive count, false negative count, candidate error cases. | Candidate-level errors are low or specific rule changes are validated. | Full question-level choice accuracy requires question-level labels, not just candidate labels. |
